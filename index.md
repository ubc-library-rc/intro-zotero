## Note: this workshop is in development and not yet complete.

Zotero is a free and open-source bibliography builder that helps you collect, organize, cite, and share your research. Create reference lists in various citation styles (APA, MLA and other formats) and organize your citations with tags and collections. Anyone can get a Zotero account for free and this workshop will tell you what you need to know to get started.  By the end of this workshop, you will know how to:

- Use Zotero to keep all your citations in order throughout your research.​
- Apply tips and tricks to get the most out of Zotero and your citation management tracking process in general. ​
- Understand how to generate a bibliography in Zotero.​
- Share citations with colleagues.​
- Assess whether Zotero is the right citation management tool for your research. ​
